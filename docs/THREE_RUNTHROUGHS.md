# Three run-throughs

This document traces exactly what the engine produces for the three
benchmark borrowers, so a reviewer can audit the reasoning without
opening the code. All figures below were generated by running
`calculateBorrowerAssessment()` against the fixtures in
`tests/scenarios/fixtures.ts` (the same fixtures the automated tests use)
- nothing here is hand-typed or hard-coded.

Where the original brief did not state a field exactly, the assumption
made to fill the `BorrowerProfile` is called out explicitly.

## Priya

### Answers as given in the brief

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Age | 29, Bengaluru |
| Employment | Salaried, software engineer at a large MNC, 5 years |
| Net income | ₹1,10,000/month |
| Existing EMI | Car loan ₹14,000/month, 2 years remaining |
| Credit score | 780 |
| Rent (essential expense) | ₹28,000/month |
| Requested | ₹8,00,000, personal loan, for a wedding |

### Assumptions made to fill gaps

- Income stability: **stable** (5 years at a large MNC, salaried) - not
  stated verbatim in the brief but a reasonable read of "software
  engineer, large MNC, 5 years."
- Recent EMI bounces: **0** (no bounce mentioned).
- Emergency savings: **unknown** (not stated).
- No collateral, no co-applicant, no existing lender offer.

### Questions shown

All 9 must questions, plus adaptive: income stability, employer tenure,
variable income share, emergency savings, credit-card utilisation,
co-applicant, lender offer. (Self-employed/informal-only questions like
ITR income, business tenure, dependents-for-informal, and collateral did
not trigger - Priya isn't self-employed and isn't buying a home/gold
loan.)

### Outputs

- **Verdict: BORROW.** "The EMI needed for ₹8,00,000 fits within your
  safe ceiling of ₹41,000/month."
- **Amount:** safe borrowing range ≈ ₹4.6L – ₹19.5L (MEDIUM confidence,
  wide because it spans the full 12-60 month tenure window); recommended
  safe starting amount ≈ ₹4.59L; indicative lender sanction ≈ ₹14.3L –
  ₹20.4L (MEDIUM confidence). The recommendation is the conservative
  lower end of the safe range, not the lender estimate.
- **Fair rate:** 9.5% – 13.15% (HIGH confidence) - driven by her 780
  score and stable income.
- **Indicative APR:** 12.6% – 16.8%, using periodic cash-flow IRR after
  the assumed 2% processing fee plus other fees over a 24-month tenure.
- **Safe EMI ceiling:** ₹41,000/month (HIGH confidence) - the FOIR-based
  capacity (50% of ₹1,10,000 usable income, minus her ₹14,000 car EMI)
  is the binding constraint; her expense-based capacity is looser
  (~₹57,000).
- **Recommended tenure:** 24 months (shortest tenure that still fits
  under the ceiling, minimising total interest).
- **Stress case:** "Your safe EMI today is about ₹41,000/month. If your
  income fell 15%, your safer ceiling would fall to about ₹32,800/month."
  This is an income-only stress; no rate shock is applied.
- **Overall confidence:** MEDIUM (driven down mainly by unknown
  emergency savings and income stated as exact-but-not-cross-verified).
- **Routing:** no redirection - personal loan is a reasonable fit.

## Ravi

### Answers as given in the brief

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Age | 42, Mysuru |
| Employment | Self-employed, kirana store, 14 years |
| Cash income | ₹40,000 – ₹80,000/month |
| ITR income | ₹4,20,000/year |
| Property | Owns shop premises, ~₹45,00,000, no existing debt on it |
| Credit score | Unknown, no formal borrowing history |
| Wife's income | ₹18,000/month |
| Requested | ₹15,00,000, for a second stock line + delivery vehicle |

### Assumptions made to fill gaps

- Household expenses: **unknown** (not stated in the brief).
- Income stability: **highly variable** (a ₹40K-80K cash-income range
  implies real month-to-month swing).
- Dependents: **2** (a reasonable household assumption; not load-bearing
  on the four headline outputs since Ravi is self-employed and the
  dependents question only affects safe amount/verdict for informal and
  self-employed borrowers indirectly via expenses, which are unknown
  here).
- Loan product initially modelled as **personal loan** (his own likely
  starting point) so the routing rule has something to redirect away
  from - this is what lets the test suite verify the routing logic
  actually fires rather than assuming Ravi already picked the "correct"
  product.
- Purpose classified as **business expansion**, productive = true.

### Questions shown

All 9 must questions, plus adaptive: income stability, business tenure,
ITR income, emergency savings, dependents (self-employed branch),
productive-purpose, expected incremental income, collateral,
co-applicant, lender offer.

### Outputs

- **Verdict: BORROW_LESS.** "The EMI needed for ₹15,00,000 (about
  ₹34,100/month) is above your safe ceiling of ₹14,400/month."
- **Amount:** safe borrowing range ≈ ₹1.6L – ₹6.9L (LOW confidence);
  recommended safe starting amount ≈ ₹1.58L; indicative
  lender sanction (for the personal-loan path he initially picked) ≈
  ₹3.4L – ₹4.9L (LOW confidence) - note this uses the *unsecured*
  income-multiple method since his selected product is unsecured. If he
  switches to the routed **loan against property** path instead, the
  sanction is materially higher because it can draw on his ₹45L shop
  collateral (verified in `tests/scenarios/scenarios.test.ts`).
- **Fair rate:** 9.5% – 16.55% (MEDIUM confidence) - a wide range driven
  by his unknown credit score (widened, not assumed poor), highly
  variable income, and documented-but-lower ITR income; partially offset
  by his collateral and his wife's income as co-applicant.
- **Indicative APR:** 10.0% – 17.05% over a 60-month recommended tenure,
  using periodic cash-flow IRR.
- **Safe EMI ceiling:** ₹14,400/month (MEDIUM confidence) - 40% FOIR
  applied to his ₹36,000 usable income (midpoint of his cash-income
  range, after a variability + range-uncertainty haircut).
- **Stress case:** "Your safe EMI today is about ₹14,400/month. If your
  income fell 15%, your safer ceiling would fall to about
  ₹14,300/month." This is an income-only stress; no rate shock is applied.
- **Overall confidence:** LOW (unknown credit score + income given as a
  range + undocumented cash income all compound).
- **Routing:** "Given your business collateral and productive purpose, a
  secured loan against property or a business loan is likely to be
  cheaper and more achievable than an unsecured personal loan." with a
  note to specifically ask about LAP/MSME schemes.

## Anita

### Answers as given in the brief

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Age | 35, Hubballi |

| Income | ₹26,000 – ₹30,000/month |

| Recent bounce | 1 EMI bounced last month |
| Requested | ₹1,50,000, electric scooter (two-wheeler loan) |

### Assumptions made to fill gaps

- Existing monthly EMI across the 3 app loans: **₹6,000/month** - the
  brief gives an outstanding *balance* (₹35,000) and an interest rate,
  not a monthly instalment; ₹6,000/month is a reasonable, moderately
  conservative estimate for short-tenure high-cost app credit at that
  balance, and is flagged as high-cost (`isHighCost: true`,
  `interestRate: 32`).
- Household expenses: **unknown** (not stated).
- Emergency savings: **0 months** (directly implied by the stress
  described - spouse unemployed 8 months, existing loans, a bounce).
- Credit score: **unknown** (not stated, and informal-income borrowers
  frequently have no bureau file).
- Purpose classified as **vehicle**, productive = **true** (the scooter
  "could increase delivery income," per the brief).

### Questions shown

All 9 must questions, plus adaptive: income stability, emergency
savings, dependents (informal branch), recent bounce (triggered because
she has existing EMIs), productive-purpose, expected incremental income.
(No collateral question - two-wheeler loan doesn't trigger it for an
informal borrower without other collateral signals.)

### Outputs

- **Verdict: DONT_BORROW.** "Your current repayment obligations already
  leave little to no safe room for a new EMI, and taking on more debt
  would add real risk of missing payments." Reasons shown: the recent
  bounce, and existing high-cost debt.
- **Amount:** safe amount = **₹0** (her existing ₹6,000 EMI already
  consumes her entire 20%-of-usable-income FOIR ceiling after the
  bounce/high-cost/low-buffer penalties are applied); indicative lender
  sanction ≈ ₹1.0L – ₹1.46L (LOW confidence) - shown explicitly as a
  *lender-side* estimate, separate from - and explicitly not a
  recommendation to use - her ₹0 safe amount.
- **Fair rate:** 9.65% – 17.75% (MEDIUM confidence) - wide because of
  unknown credit score, highly variable income, existing high-cost debt,
  and the recent bounce, all pushing the upper bound up.
- **Indicative APR:** 11.5% – 20.9%, using periodic cash-flow IRR.
- **Safe EMI ceiling: ₹0/month.** FOIR ceiling starts at the informal
  base of 35%, then the high-cost-debt (-5pp), recent-bounce (-7pp), and
  low-buffer (-3pp) penalties bring it down to 20%; 20% of her ₹16,800
  usable income (₹28,000 midpoint, haircut for range + high variability)
  minus her existing ₹6,000 EMI is ≤ 0, floored at ₹0.
- **Stress case:** "Your safe EMI today is about ₹0/month. If your
  income fell 15%, your safer ceiling would fall to about ₹0/month." -
  correctly shows there is no further room to lose. This is an income-only
  stress; no rate shock is applied.
- **Overall confidence:** MEDIUM (several fields are estimated, but the
  risk flags themselves - the bounce, the high-cost debt - are directly
  stated by the borrower, not inferred, which keeps confidence from
  collapsing to LOW).
- **Productive purpose acknowledged but not decisive:** `isProductivePurpose`
  is `true` and the reasons list would mention it under a BORROW/BORROW_LESS
  verdict, but the severe-stress check runs first and overrides it -
  exactly the brief's requirement that "the productive potential of the
  scooter should be acknowledged but must not automatically override
  current repayment stress."
- **Routing:** no redirection suggested (no usable collateral, and a
  two-wheeler loan is already a reasonably-priced secured option for her
  situation).
